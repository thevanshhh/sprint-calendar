# Sprint Calendar — Product Context

## Purpose & Goal
A hyper-focused founder sprint calendar and execution dashboard designed to manage daily hour-by-hour operational momentum toward two core objectives:
1. **Financial Target:** Generate **$2,000 cash** within the sprint window (October 9 to November 30).
2. **Physical Recomposition Target:** 8-Week Body Recomposition: Reduce body fat while building/maintaining muscle. Starting at age 21, 170 cm, 76 kg, untrained beginner, 100% vegetarian without protein powder, using 2 × 4 kg + 2 × 2.5 kg dumbbells. Primary indicators: waist reduction at navel, muscle definition, strength, stamina, and a leaner face (not just scale weight).

## Operating Context
- **User:** Solo founder / operator executing high-leverage daily routines (business audits, in-person walk-in business discovery, outbound emails, physical health, skill building, and content creation).
- **Daily Operating Rhythm:**
  - Day starts at 8:00 AM with wake-up, physical reset, and breakfast.
  - Morning focus: Go Leverage audit production & delivery.
  - Afternoon focus: Walk-in merchant discovery (10 genuine conversations/day).
  - Evening focus: Appointy email prospecting and follow-ups.
  - Night focus: Decompression walk, book reading, camera practice, reel creation, skill building, and daily market intelligence synthesis.
  - Hard stop at 2:00 AM.
- **Operating Day Boundary:** Uses a 4:00 AM day cutover (`adj = new Date(now.getTime() - 4 * 3600 * 1000)`) to correctly align late-night productivity (up to 2 AM) with the intended calendar day.

## Fitness & Body Recomposition Protocol (8 Weeks)
- **Daily Targets:**
  - Calories: 1,750–1,850 kcal
  - Protein: 100–110 g (Soy chunks, Amul dahi, dal, tofu, roasted chana)
  - Water: 2.5–3.0 L
  - Steps: 8,000–10,000
  - Sleep: 7–9 hours
  - Cooking oil limit: 3–4 tsp/day
- **Workout Split:**
  - Weeks 1–2 (Foundation): Full Body A / Walks / Full Body B / Walks & Mobility / Full Body A / 45–60m walk / Sunday Rest.
  - Weeks 3–8 (Hypertrophy & Conditioning): Push + Run / Pull / Legs + Abs / Push + Run / Pull / Legs + 5-Round Conditioning / Sunday Rest.
- **Weekly Sunday Measurement Audit:**
  - Fasted measurements: Weight, Waist at navel, Chest, Arm, Thigh, Front/Side/Back photos.
  - Waist at navel is primary indicator of body fat loss.

## Core Constraints
- **Offline & Local-First:** All schedules, checklist completions, scoreboard tallies, and Sunday measurement audits persist in browser `localStorage` (`KEY: "sprint-cal-v3"`) and sync across devices.
- **Fast Execution:** No slow backend or login barriers; instantly available on desktop and mobile browsers.
