      Road to $2,000

      • Oct 12–18
        10 real conversations a daySame questions every time. Log every business and count the problems each night.
      • Oct 19–25
        Pick the offerTake the 1 or 2 problems that came up most. Write one clear offer with one price.
      • Oct 26–Nov 15
        Sell itGo back to every interviewed owner with the offer. Keep the 10 daily conversations going.
      • Nov 16–30
        Deliver and collectFinish the work, take payment. For example, 4 clients at $500 or 8 at $250 reaches $2,000.

      Check the total every Sunday. If it is under a quarter of the goal by Nov 8, spend more of the walk-in block on follow-ups with owners you already met.

      Walk-in conversation flow

      • What exactly do you guys do?
      • How do most of your customers find you?
      • When someone shows interest, what happens next?
      • Where do you usually lose potential customers?
      • What takes up a lot of your time that you wish was easier?
      • How often does that happen, and what happens when it does?
      • Only then: if I could help fix that, would you want to look at it?

      Discover first, sell second. After 20 conversations review the patterns, after 50 review again, after 100 the repeated problems become your offer.

      Eating for 66 kg

      • Protein at every meal: eggs, dal, paneer, curd, chicken or soya.
      • Keep roti and rice to a measured portion, and fill the rest of the plate with sabzi and salad.
      • Drop sugary drinks and packaged snacks. Chai with less sugar is fine.
      • Carry a banana, peanuts or roasted chana for the walk-in afternoons so you don't buy snacks on the street.
      • Move daily: the morning workout plus the evening walk covers it.

      Losing 4 kg over 3 months is about 1.3 kg a month. Weigh in once a week, Sunday morning, same conditions.

      Rules that keep it working

      • One excellent audit beats five garbage audits.
      • Ten real conversations, not ten businesses entered or ten cards collected.
      • No business work while eating, and no scrolling in the morning.
      • Learn what the market told you today, not what a video recommends.
      • At 2 AM you are done. Don't start another task.
      • If you have college, move the walk-ins around it and leave the audit block alone.
